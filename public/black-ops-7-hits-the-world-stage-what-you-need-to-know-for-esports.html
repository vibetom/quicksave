Black Ops 7 Hits the World Stage: What You Need to Know for Esports World Cup 2026

The Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 tournament is live at the Esports World Cup 2026 with a $1.8 million prize pool and a full roster of matches starting today.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Aug 05, 2026 · 10:58 UTC 3 MIN

The virtual arenas are heating up as the Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 tournament officially kicks off today, August 5th, at the Esports World Cup 2026. Fans can buckle up for a high-stakes competition with a hefty $1.8 million prize pool on the line.

Sixteen qualified teams are set to battle it out in this premier event. Today marks the debut for twelve of these formidable squads, while the remaining four will jump into the fray tomorrow, August 6th. The tournament's intensity will build over five days, concluding on August 9th. The initial Group Stage runs from August 5th to August 7th, setting the stage for the dramatic Playoffs scheduled for August 8th and 9th.

Today's opening fixtures promise a thrilling start to the competition. In Group A, the action begins at 14:00 BST/15:00 CEST with Movistar KOI clashing with the Carolina Royal Ravens. Following that, FaZe Clan will face off against The Pit at 15:15 BST/16:15 CEST. G2 Esports and Cloud9 are slated for a matchup at 16:30 BST/17:30 CEST, while Gentle Mates will take on OMiT at 17:45 BST/18:45 CEST.

Group B also features some anticipated showdowns on day one. OpTic Gaming will go head-to-head with Team WaR at 19:00 BST/20:00 CEST, and 100 Thieves will challenge Boston Breach at 20:15 BST/21:15 CEST.

As the Group Stage progresses, more teams will enter the fray. Tomorrow, August 6th, Group B continues with Team Falcons meeting Project Notorious at 14:00 BST/15:00 CEST, and Team Heretics facing Vancouver Surge at the same time. The path to the $1.8 million prize is clear, and the race for supremacy in Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 has officially begun.
