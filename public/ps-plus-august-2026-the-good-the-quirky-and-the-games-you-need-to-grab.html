PS Plus August 2026: The Good, The Quirky, And The Games You Need To Grab Now

Sony's new monthly games drop includes a AAA zombie epic and an indie darling, but don't forget the popular titles making their exit.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Jul 28, 2026 · 18:53 UTC 3 MIN

Sony has officially unveiled the PlayStation Plus monthly games lineup for August 2026, delivering a fresh wave of titles for subscribers. This new batch features a blend of open-world action, cooperative adventure, and atmospheric horror, arriving just as several other notable games are reportedly preparing their departure from the service.

Leading the charge for August is Dying Light 2 Stay Human: Reloaded Edition, a post-apocalyptic parkour and combat epic available for both PlayStation 5 and PlayStation 4. Joining it are Big Walk, a cooperative exploration game exclusively for PlayStation 5, and Signalis, a critically acclaimed survival horror title on PlayStation 4. These games will be accessible to PlayStation Plus members from Tuesday, August 4, 2026, and will remain available until Monday, August 31, 2026, as confirmed by PlayStation.Blog.

While we welcome these new additions, it's also a crucial time for players to look at the other side of the ledger. The collection of games for July 2026, featuring a modern military shooter, a turn-based RPG, and an action RPG, will only be available until August 3. This short overlap gives subscribers a brief window to claim any remaining titles from July before they disappear.

Adding to the dynamic nature of subscription services, a significant number of titles are reportedly leaving the broader PlayStation Plus game catalog in August. According to TrueTrophies, nine games are set to exit, with prominent titles such as the zombie-slaying Dead Island 2 and the tactical RPG Unicorn Overlord among them. This reported exodus underscores the ever-shifting landscape of digital libraries, making timely engagement key for subscribers.

This blend of incoming and outgoing titles highlights the constant churn within subscription gaming. On one hand, players are gaining diverse experiences, from the sprawling cityscapes of Dying Light 2 to the tense corridors of Signalis. On the other, the reported departure of popular games like Dead Island 2 and Unicorn Overlord serves as a clear signal for players to prioritize their backlogs and ensure they experience these titles before they're potentially gone. It’s a classic subscription tightrope walk: balance the new with what’s about to vanish, making every play session count.
