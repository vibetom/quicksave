Overwatch And Warzone Bring The Heat (And The Cash) To Esports World Cup Week 4

Two of gaming's biggest titles are battling it out for million-dollar prize pools as the Esports World Cup 2026 enters a critical week.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Jul 24, 2026 · 21:34 UTC 3 MIN

The fourth week of the Esports World Cup (EWC) 2026 has officially commenced, ushering in major tournaments for both Overwatch and Call of Duty: Warzone. Both events are unfolding at the Paris Expo Porte de Versailles, adding a significant competitive pulse to the global esports celebration.

The Overwatch Champions Series 2026 Midseason Championship is set to run from July 29 to August 2, featuring a robust $1,000,000 prize pool. The top team will walk away with a substantial $400,000, a sum that underscores the game's recent competitive momentum. Overwatch esports has been enjoying a resurgent year in 2026, with strong viewership recorded across Southeast Asia, MENA, and North America, even if peak viewership from 2018 remains the highest point.

Meanwhile, the Call of Duty: Warzone Resurgence Series 2026 Championship is also taking center stage, serving as the grand finale of the new 'Resurgence Series' Call of Duty circuit. This tournament, running from July 29 to August 1, also boasts an impressive $1,000,000 prize pool, with the victorious squad securing $250,000.

These tournaments are key components of the broader Esports World Cup 2026, which opened in Paris on July 8 and will continue through August 23. The EWC brings together more than 2,000 top competitors and 200 esports organizations from over 100 countries, transforming Paris into a global hub for competitive gaming. For both Overwatch and Warzone, the EWC provides an unparalleled platform, elevating their competitive scenes and attracting significant attention from fans worldwide.
