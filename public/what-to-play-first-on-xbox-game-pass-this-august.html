What to Play First on Xbox Game Pass This August

Microsoft just dropped the first wave of August 2026 Game Pass titles, featuring an acclaimed day-one RPG, a classic shooter bundle, and a highly anticipated beta.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Aug 04, 2026 · 16:26 UTC 3 MIN

August is kicking off with a bang for Xbox Game Pass subscribers, as Microsoft unveils the first ten titles joining the service this month. This initial wave brings a compelling mix of brand-new releases, an exciting beta, and definitive re-releases for Premium plan holders, ensuring a varied menu for all players.

Leading the charge is Game Freak's action RPG, Beast of Reincarnation, which landed on Game Pass today, August 4. This highly anticipated title is a day-one release, available across Cloud, Xbox Series X|S, Handheld, and PC. Early reviews are already calling it a "must-play," making it a prime candidate for your immediate gaming attention.

For those who appreciate a trip down memory lane with a modern polish, Game Pass Premium subscribers also have a treat today. Heretic + Hexen, a definitive re-release of the beloved "dark spell-casting shooters," is now available. This bundle offers a chance to experience these classics on Cloud, Console, Handheld, and PC, perfect for fans of vintage first-person action.

Looking ahead just a couple of days, the Gears of War: E-Day Open Beta is set to storm onto the scene. Starting August 6, Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass subscribers can dive into the "Horde Siege PvE" mode, offering an early taste of the next chapter in the iconic Gears saga.

Beyond these immediate highlights, the month promises even more. Keep an eye out for Cricket 26 and Date Everything!, both slated to join the Game Pass library later in August. These additions round out a diverse initial offering, catering to sports enthusiasts and those looking for something a bit different.

While July saw 14 new titles arrive, this first August lineup includes ten additions—three entirely new games and seven expansions to the Premium plan. Despite the slightly smaller quantity in this initial batch, the quality and variety of the new and featured titles make it a strong start to the month, providing plenty of fresh experiences for Game Pass subscribers.
