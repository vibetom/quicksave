What Just Happened At The Esports World Cup 2026?

Paris crowned champions in Dota 2, Free Fire, and more as the Esports World Cup wraps up its main events and gears up for a free fan celebration.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Jul 27, 2026 · 16:38 UTC 4 MIN

The Esports World Cup 2026 recently concluded its second week of major tournaments in Paris, paving the way for a large-scale fan celebration. At the Paris Expo Porte de Versailles, champions were crowned in several prominent titles, including Dota 2 and Free Fire.

The competitive atmosphere crackled with intensity as teams vied for honor and significant financial rewards. In one notable championship match, Team Vitality secured a 4-2 series win, earning a $150,000 grand prize. The Free Fire tournament saw LYON deliver a standout performance across seven Finals matches, ultimately overcoming eleven other finalists to seize the championship title.

The Dota 2 championship concluded with PVISION defeating BB Team in a compelling four-game series. PVISION bounced back after losing the initial game, triumphing in a lengthy Game 2 before securing the series victory. Elsewhere, Team Vision bested Aurora Gaming with a clean 3-0 sweep to claim the Teamfight Tactics trophy, collecting $150,000 and 1,000 Club Championship points.

Beyond these headline-grabbing victories, the EWC 2026 hosted other high-stakes competitions. The PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS tournament, held from July 21-26, featured 24 teams competing for a $2 million prize pool, along with Club Championship points and qualification for the PUBG Global Series. Additionally, the FC Pro World Championship showcased 36 of the world's top EA SPORTS FC 26 players at the Paris Expo, highlighting elite talent in the realm of sports simulation.

To cap off these intense competitions, the Esports World Cup will host a complimentary outdoor gathering celebrating esports, gaming, and various forms of entertainment. This event, scheduled for August 2nd, will unfold in the heart of Paris, inviting enthusiasts to participate in the festivities.

The conclusion of the main events at the Esports World Cup 2026 underscores the rapidly expanding global presence of competitive gaming. From the diverse array of game titles to the exceptional skill displayed by participants, the EWC illuminates the thrilling connection between top-tier competition and enthusiastic community engagement.
