Fortnite Kicks Off Euro 2024: What To Expect In-Game

Epic Games brings the excitement of the ongoing UEFA Euro 2024 football championship to Fortnite with new experiences and cosmetics.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Jul 28, 2026 · 21:36 UTC 2 MIN

Fortnite is once again blurring the lines between the virtual world and real-life culture, this time by partnering with UEFA to bring the ongoing Euro 2024 football (soccer) championship directly into the game. With the tournament in full swing, this collaboration lands with perfect timing, allowing players to dive into the excitement from within the Fortnite universe.

This isn't just a simple branding exercise. The partnership is set to introduce a range of in-game experiences, fresh cosmetic items, and themed events designed to immerse players in the spirit of Euro 2024. From the roar of the crowd to the thrill of competition, Epic Games is making sure the championship feels alive inside Fortnite.

This move aligns perfectly with Epic Games' established strategy. We've seen Fortnite consistently integrate major real-world events and brands, spanning from chart-topping music artists to blockbuster film franchises. This ongoing fusion keeps the game feeling dynamic, relevant, and consistently refreshed, offering something new for its vast player base.

While the full roster of event details is still being unveiled, reports suggest players can anticipate a wave of challenges tied to the tournament. Expect to see new skins inspired by the participating national teams, along with emotes that let you celebrate like your favorite iconic players. These additions are designed to deepen the connection between the in-game action and the real-world spectacle of Euro 2024.

Ultimately, this collaboration serves a dual purpose for Epic Games. It provides a fresh stream of content to keep existing players engaged and invested, while simultaneously acting as a magnet for new players—especially football enthusiasts—who might be drawn into Fortnite's expansive world by the appeal of a major sporting event. It’s another smart play in Epic’s playbook, ensuring Fortnite remains a central hub where digital entertainment meets global culture.
