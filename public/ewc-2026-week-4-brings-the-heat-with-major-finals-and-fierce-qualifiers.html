EWC 2026: Week 4 Brings the Heat With Major Finals and Fierce Qualifiers

From Call of Duty: Warzone's climactic finish to the start of Overwatch 2's Midseason Championship, here's what you need to know about the biggest battles happening now.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Jul 30, 2026 · 16:05 UTC 3 MIN

Week four of the Esports World Cup 2026 is upon us, and the intensity is dialed all the way up. We're witnessing the culmination of some tournaments, the beginning of others, and critical qualifiers that will shape the rest of the competitive season. It's a concentrated blast of high-stakes esports action, offering defining moments across multiple top-tier titles.

The battleground for Call of Duty: Warzone is reaching its explosive finale. Thirty-two teams are currently clashing for a share of the impressive $1,000,000 prize pool. The stakes are immense, with the finals employing the high-tension Match Point format to crown a champion. Expect tactical plays and nail-biting finishes as competitors vie for glory.

Meanwhile, the Overwatch Champions Series Midseason Championship is just getting started, kicking off its group stage this week, running from July 29-31. Sixteen teams are set to compete in two GSL-style double-elimination groups, a format known for its intense matchups. As the tournament progresses to its single-elimination playoffs, a new tactical layer will emerge: hero bans. This will lead to strategic drafting and adaptable team compositions, all building towards a best-of-seven Grand Final on August 2.

On the mobile front, the Mobile Legends: Bang Bang Mid Season Cup (MSC) is entering its decisive phase. The remaining eight teams are locked into a single-elimination bracket, each one fighting to claim a piece of the substantial $3,000,000 prize pool. It's a winner-take-all scenario where every match could send a team packing. Additionally, the Street Fighter 6 tournament is also heating up, bringing together 32 of the globe's best players to compete for a $1,000,000 prize pool, promising electrifying one-on-one combat.

Looking ahead, Week 4 also marks the launch of the Honor of Kings tournament, introducing another major title into the EWC fray. For fans of fighting games, the TEKKEN 8 Last Chance Qualifier is providing an opportunity for hundreds of competitors to battle through an open bracket. Only four coveted spots in the main event are available, making for a truly cutthroat competition. Similarly, Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 is hosting its own Last Chance Qualifier, where teams will navigate a double-elimination bracket to secure one final spot.

This week truly embodies the spirit of the Esports World Cup 2026, delivering a packed schedule of competitive gaming. From million-dollar finales to the desperate fights for qualification, Week 4 is proving to be a defining period for several esports titles and their dedicated communities, as reported by DAZN News US.
