What You Missed (And Can Still Catch) At The Esports World Cup 2026

From Overwatch to Street Fighter 6, the EWC in Paris is heating up with million-dollar prize pools and intense competition across multiple titles.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Jul 31, 2026 · 21:35 UTC 3 MIN

The Esports World Cup 2026, unfolding in Paris, France, is now in its fourth week, delivering a cascade of high-stakes competition across some of gaming's biggest titles. With several major tournaments either kicking off or barreling towards their conclusions, the action is thick with million-dollar prize pools on the line.

Fans of tactical hero shooters have been locked into the Overwatch Champions Series (OWCS) 2026 Midseason Championship. The group stage wrapped up between July 29 and 31, and now the event is deep into its playoffs, which run through August 2. The winning teams will claim their share of a substantial $1,000,000 prize pool, making every elimination a critical moment.

The battle royale landscape is equally charged, with the Call of Duty: Warzone Resurgence Series 2026 Championship. Kicking off on July 30 and continuing until August 2, this tournament features 32 teams vying for supremacy and a $1,000,000 prize pool. The competition is fierce as squads navigate the high-octane maps.

For mobile gaming enthusiasts, the Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB) Mid Season Cup (MSC) Playoffs ignited on July 29, promising some of the most intense action of the entire World Cup. This event boasts a staggering $3,000,000 prize pool, a significant incentive for the top-tier MLBB teams to leave everything on the virtual arena floor.

Fighting game aficionados are also getting their fill. Street Fighter 6 at EWC 26 entered its Group Stage during Week 4, showcasing 32 of the world's best players as they begin their quest for a $1,000,000 prize. Similarly, TEKKEN 8 at EWC 26 began its Last Chance Qualifier on July 31, where competitors are fighting tooth and nail for one of four coveted spots in the main event. Over in the mobile MOBA space, Honor of Kings (KWC) at EWC 26 commenced its Play-Ins from July 30-31, with the Group Stage set to follow from August 1-4.

With so many critical junctures unfolding simultaneously, the Esports World Cup 2026 is proving to be a true spectacle of competitive gaming, drawing eyes to Paris for its climactic finishes.
