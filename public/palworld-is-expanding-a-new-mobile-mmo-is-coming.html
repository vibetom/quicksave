Palworld Is Expanding: A New Mobile MMO Is Coming

Garena and Pocketpair are teaming up for Palworld Online, a completely new MMORPG built exclusively for your phone, arriving in 2026.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Aug 05, 2026 · 13:09 UTC 3 MIN

Get ready to catch Pals on the go, because a brand new chapter for the Palworld universe is on the horizon. Garena, with an official license from developer Pocketpair, has announced the development and publishing of Palworld Online, an MMORPG crafted exclusively for mobile devices. This fresh take on the creature-collecting phenomenon is slated for release in 2026.

Palworld Online is not just a port; it's being built from the ground up as a distinct experience for iOS and Android. Players can look forward to a new narrative setting, complete with original story elements, expanding the lore into a full Massively Multiplayer Online ecosystem that stands apart from the existing Palworld title.

The game promises a seamless, interconnected open world ripe for exploration and creature collecting. Key features include both PvE and PvP online play, co-op capabilities, and the exciting prospect of creating a shared home base where multiple players can gather. It sounds like an expansive social hub for Pal-taming adventures.

Understanding the demands of mobile gaming, Palworld Online will feature a streamlined, touch-friendly interface, offering optional one-tap controls to ensure a smooth and accessible experience for handheld play. This deliberate design choice highlights Garena's focus on a tailored mobile experience rather than a direct translation of the PC/console game.

Pocketpair CEO Takuro Mizobe expressed enthusiasm for the collaboration, citing Garena's extensive experience in global publishing, localization, and live operations within the mobile gaming space. This partnership hints at a robust support system for Palworld Online once it launches.

While official details about the game's business model have yet to be revealed, Insider Gaming speculates that Palworld Online will adopt a free-to-play model, supported by microtransactions. This approach would align with Garena's track record and expertise with similar titles in the mobile market, making the game accessible to a wide audience when it arrives.
