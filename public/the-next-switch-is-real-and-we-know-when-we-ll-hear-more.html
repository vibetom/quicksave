The Next Switch Is Real, And We Know When We'll Hear More

Nintendo officially confirms its 'Switch successor' will be announced by March 2025, but don't expect details at the June Direct.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Jul 26, 2026 · 12:36 UTC 3 MIN

The waiting game for Nintendo fans just got a little more official. Nintendo has confirmed that its next-generation console, referred to as a "Switch successor," will be unveiled before the fiscal year concludes on March 31, 2025. This declaration moves the conversation beyond the realm of pure rumor, setting a firm timeline for when we'll finally get a look at the next chapter in Nintendo's hardware lineup.

While the confirmation is exciting, eager players should temper their expectations for the immediate future. A Nintendo Direct is on the horizon for June 2024, set to focus exclusively on software releases for the current Switch platform slated for the latter half of the year. Crucially, Nintendo has explicitly stated this upcoming presentation will not include any information about the successor. This deliberate exclusion builds anticipation, signaling that a dedicated, standalone event for the new console is likely in the cards.

Although official details are still under wraps, various reports and rumors have circulated, painting a picture of what this new hardware might offer. Among the more prominent whispers are claims of a custom Nvidia chip at its core, potentially bringing improved ray tracing capabilities to the next system. If these reports hold true, it suggests a significant leap in graphical performance for Nintendo's next console, offering a more immersive experience than its predecessor.

Alongside potential hardware upgrades, early speculation also points to a robust software launch. Reports suggest that a new flagship 3D Mario adventure and an upcoming Pokémon entry could be among the games leading the charge for the new system. These iconic franchises have historically served as pillars for Nintendo console launches, and their rumored presence suggests a familiar strategy for drawing in a massive audience from day one.

Despite the clear announcement window, many key details remain elusive. Specifics regarding a release date, the console's price, and its full technical specifications are still unconfirmed. However, the official confirmation of an announcement within the current fiscal year provides a concrete timeframe, allowing us to anticipate when Nintendo will pull back the curtain on its highly anticipated next-generation device. The curtain is rising, slowly but surely, on what comes next for the Switch line.
