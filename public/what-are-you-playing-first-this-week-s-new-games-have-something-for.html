What Are You Playing First? This Week's New Games Have Something For Everyone

From a chill adventure with friends to a 4v4 tag-team fighting game, August 3-9 is packed with fresh experiences.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Aug 03, 2026 · 19:02 UTC 3 MIN

This week, the gaming landscape unfurls a surprisingly diverse tapestry of new titles and content, offering a little something for every kind of player. Whether your ideal escape involves cozy co-op, intense brawling, or deep RPG mechanics, the releases hitting storefronts between August 3rd and 9th look ready to deliver.

For those seeking a more relaxed pace, House House, the studio behind beloved indie hits, is launching `Big Walk` on August 4th, according to ScreenHub. This adventure title promises a laid-back, cooperative experience, perfect for friends looking to explore new digital horizons together without the pressure of a ticking clock or a demanding leaderboard.

Shifting gears, RPG enthusiasts have a couple of intriguing options. Game Freak's `Beast of Reincarnation`, an action RPG set in a post-apocalyptic Japan, also arrives on August 4th, as reported by ScreenHub. Meanwhile, for those still engrossed in their creature-collecting journey, the `Pokemon Pokopia Expansion Pass Part 1` drops on August 5th, bringing new content to explore, as noted by DLCompare.com.

Players on Nintendo's latest hardware have reason to celebrate, too. `Lies of P: Complete Edition` makes its debut on the Nintendo Switch 2 on August 6th, bundling the acclaimed base game with its `Overture` expansion, according to ScreenHub. This offers a chance for new audiences to experience the dark, re-imagined fairy tale that captivated many upon its initial release.

Fighting game fans also have a busy week ahead. The Marvel universe expands its digital footprint with `Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls`, a 4v4 tag-team brawler featuring 20 iconic Marvel characters, launching on August 6th, as reported by ScreenHub. And for those already deep in the competitive scene, `Street Fighter 6 - Yasmine` introduces new content to the popular fighting title on August 3rd, available across Xbox Series X, PlayStation 4, Switch 2, Xbox One, and PlayStation 5, per DLCompare.com.

Finally, for players craving a narrative twist, `Kill The Shadow`, a moody detective adventure, is set to release on August 4th. DLCompare.com highlights this title as an option for those looking to delve into a more atmospheric and story-driven experience. With such a varied slate, this week truly feels like an open invitation to discover something fresh and exciting.
