What's At Stake at the Esports World Cup? Everything.

As the OWCS Midseason Championship and MLBB Mid Season Cup Playoffs heat up, teams aren't just battling for millions in prizes, but also crucial points for ultimate glory.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Jul 29, 2026 · 11:00 UTC 4 MIN

The Esports World Cup is now in full swing, turning up the heat on two major tournaments this week: the Overwatch Champions Series Midseason Championship and the Mobile Legends: Bang Bang Mid Season Cup Playoffs. With substantial prize pools, coveted titles, and vital Club Championship points on the line, the stakes couldn't be higher for the world's elite esports teams.

From July 29 to August 2, the OWCS Midseason Championship is gathering 16 of the planet's top Overwatch teams. These contenders aren't just vying for the prestige of being named OWCS 2026 Midseason Champion, but also a share of a formidable $1,000,000 prize pool and crucial Club Championship points, as confirmed by the Esports World Cup's official website.

Meanwhile, the Mobile Legends: Bang Bang Mid Season Cup Playoffs continue their run, drawing the best MLBB teams globally. This segment of the EWC promises intense competition for a colossal $3,000,000 prize pool, the sought-after MSC trophy, and, of course, those all-important Club Championship points. The Esports World Cup's official site highlights that these ongoing tournaments mark the halfway point in the broader race for the overall Club Championship.

Beyond the immediate thrill of victory, the Esports World Cup is also bringing broader industry narratives into focus. Esports Insider reported on North American MLBB pros like 'Ashlay' expressing a strong desire for more regional tournaments, underscoring a notable disparity in competitive opportunities. Furthermore, Team Vitality's MLBB roster, an all-women team, is challenging preconceived notions about mobile gaming and women in esports, reportedly establishing itself as one of the most successful competitive gaming teams.

During the event, Ray Ng from Moonton, the developers behind MLBB, shared insights with Esports Insider regarding the game's presence at the EWC, efforts to cultivate mobile esports in Western markets, and the potential of AI coaching tools.

Looking back at champions already crowned, Virtus.pro secured the PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS trophy, while Razvan 'RvPLegend' Puiu claimed victory in the FC Pro World Championship, according to the Esports World Cup official website. These early successes set a high bar for the remaining competitions, ensuring that every match across the diverse lineup of titles contributes to a grander narrative of excellence and ambition at the Esports World Cup.
