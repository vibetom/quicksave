Sony CFO Reaffirms Digital-Only Future Amidst Player Backlash

Despite widespread community concern and a planned boycott, Sony's CFO indicates the company is committed to its physical game phase-out.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Aug 06, 2026 · 13:09 UTC 3 MIN

Sony's announcement in early June about discontinuing physical PlayStation games starting in January 2028 has ignited a significant response from the player community. This move, which charts a course toward a digital-only future for the console, has become a hot topic among fans.

According to Notebookcheck, players have voiced considerable negative feedback regarding the shift. Core concerns revolve around the perceived erosion of game ownership, the eventual disappearance of the used-game market, and a heightened dependency on the PlayStation Store for accessing titles.

In a clear sign of community pushback, a large-scale boycott is reportedly being organized for August 2026. This coordinated effort aims to persuade Sony to reconsider its decision and potentially reverse the course it has set for physical media.

However, it appears Sony is holding firm. Despite the growing opposition and threats of a boycott, the company seems unwavering in its commitment to the digital-first strategy, as reported by Notebookcheck.

During Sony's recent earnings presentation, CFO Lin Tao directly addressed the mounting criticism. Tao confirmed the company's dedication to the strategy, explaining that the decision was reached after extensive deliberation and careful consideration, and the company intends to proceed thoughtfully. This statement, reported by Notebookcheck, underscores the internal deliberation behind the decision.

Tao acknowledged the deep emotional connection many players have with their games and the personal memories associated with them. While expressing an understanding of these sentiments, the CFO did not suggest a potential return to physical discs. Instead, Tao indicated that Sony would continue exploring methods to integrate these players into its evolving digital ecosystem, reinforcing the company's forward momentum.
