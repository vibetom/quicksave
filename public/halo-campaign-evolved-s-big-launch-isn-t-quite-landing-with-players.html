Halo: Campaign Evolved's Big Launch Isn't Quite Landing with Players

New Halo arrives with Game Pass access and fresh missions, but early reactions point to a bumpy ride. Meanwhile, Bethesda talks future, calming Elder Scrolls concerns.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Jul 24, 2026 · 18:55 UTC 3 MIN

A new chapter in the Master Chief saga, Halo: Campaign Evolved, has officially launched, landing on July 28, 2026. Eager players got a head start with up to five days of early access, beginning July 23, diving into what's been described as a re-imagining or remake of the beloved franchise.

The game is broadly available, reaching fans on Xbox, Steam, and PlayStation. Adding a significant boost to accessibility, Halo: Campaign Evolved also arrived on day one with both Xbox Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass, inviting a massive audience to jump in immediately.

This latest installment introduces new narrative threads through 'Operation: METEORITE,' a set of three fresh missions starring Master Chief and another sergeant, expanding the established universe. The promise of new adventures for iconic characters is a clear draw for long-time fans and newcomers alike.

However, the initial reception from players has been a bit turbulent. Early reviews are reportedly 'tottering,' with a chorus of fan complaints echoing across platforms. Players have specifically called out 'Bugs, pointless logins, and duff servers' as significant hurdles to their enjoyment, suggesting a less-than-smooth launch experience for many.

In other major industry news, Bethesda has stepped forward to offer some clarity regarding its highly anticipated titles. Following recent company layoffs, fans expressed concerns about the fate of upcoming projects. Bethesda has now confirmed that these workforce changes have not impacted 'the roadmap' for The Elder Scrolls 6.

Furthermore, Bethesda used the opportunity to reassure its community that Oblivion Remastered has not been cast aside. The company confirmed that the remastered classic remains firmly on its radar, a piece of news likely to please fans who have been patiently awaiting an updated return to Cyrodiil.
