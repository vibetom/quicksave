Saudi PIF Confirms EA Acquisition: What It Means For The Future of Gaming

The $55 billion deal cements a growing trend of Saudi investment in major gaming and esports properties.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Jul 31, 2026 · 18:50 UTC 2 MIN

Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund (PIF) is set to finalize its acquisition of Electronic Arts next week, a move valued at $55 billion. This development, which confirms prior rumors, represents a significant moment for the gaming industry, as reported by outlets like PC Gamer and GameSpot.

This isn't an isolated event, but rather a prominent piece in a larger mosaic. The acquisition of EA falls within a broader, confirmed trend of Saudi-backed groups expanding their footprint across nearly every level of the esports and gaming industry throughout the first half of 2026, according to Esports Insider.

Indeed, the PIF's investment in Electronic Arts follows a series of other substantial moves by Saudi-backed entities. These include the ownership of the prominent tournament organizer ESL FACEIT Group. Furthermore, Moonton, the developer behind the mobile sensation Mobile Legends: Bang Bang, was acquired for over $6 billion by a Saudi-backed group. Esports Insider also noted that RTS, a company under the umbrella of a Saudi investment entity, gained complete control of the venerable EVO tournament series in February 2026.

The integration of a major publisher like Electronic Arts into this evolving landscape marks a significant acceleration of Saudi-backed entities' strategic expansion across the global gaming and esports landscape.
