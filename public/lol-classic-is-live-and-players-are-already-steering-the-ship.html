LoL Classic is Live, And Players Are Already Steering the Ship

Riot's nostalgic reboot brings back old Summoner's Rift and original champions, but its most exciting feature is the player-led 'Council Voting' system that dictates future content.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Jul 29, 2026 · 13:12 UTC 2 MIN

League of Legends Classic is officially live, inviting players to step back in time. Launched on July 29, 2026, this new take on the venerable MOBA offers a direct portal to a simpler era, featuring the original Summoner's Rift map, a roster of around 60 champions with their classic kits, and the return of the familiar old rune system. For many, it's a chance to relive the early days of Runeterra.

But this isn't merely a trip down memory lane; it's a living, evolving experience. Riot Games has already unveiled a comprehensive roadmap for future content, with updates slated to arrive every two weeks. We won't have to wait long for the first additions: new champions Akali, Kennen, and Shen are confirmed to join the fray with Patch 26.16, according to Sheep Esports.

The real game-changer, however, is on the horizon. Starting with Patch 26.17, which is expected on August 26, the 'Council Voting' system will be introduced. This innovative feature puts the reins directly into the hands of the player base, empowering them to vote on upcoming content. From new champions to skin releases and even fundamental gameplay features, the community will now have a direct say in how League of Legends Classic evolves.

This community-driven approach marks a significant departure for Riot in managing a live game. By entrusting players with such direct influence over development, the 'Council Voting' system has the potential to set a new benchmark for player engagement in long-running titles. It transforms a nostalgic revisit into a dynamic, player-shaped world, truly allowing the community to steer the ship into its future.
