Why Adidas Is Giving Over 100 Countries Custom Kits For The Esports Nations Cup

The inaugural nation-based esports event just locked in a major global partner, setting the stage for a new level of national pride in competitive gaming.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Jul 30, 2026 · 21:41 UTC 2 MIN

A significant partnership just dropped for the inaugural Esports Nations Cup (ENC), as the Esports Foundation and Adidas have announced that Adidas will be the Official Kit Partner and a Global Partner for the event. This landmark collaboration, revealed on July 30, 2026, aims to infuse competitive gaming with the same visual prestige and national identity long associated with traditional sports at a global scale. The first-ever ENC is slated to kick off on November 2, 2026, in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.

Adidas is set to design and produce custom competition apparel for more than 100 participating countries and territories. This isn't just a generic uniform; we're talking about uniquely tailored jerseys and tracksuits for each delegation, covering approximately 2,000 athletes and coaches. The entire initiative is designed to highlight national identity while championing the global camaraderie that defines esports.

Mohammed Al Nimer, Chief Commercial Officer of the Esports Foundation, emphasized the core ambition behind this partnership: to give esports athletes the chance to represent their home nations with the same gravitas and honor seen across major global athletic events. It's about bringing that deep sense of national pride to the digital arena, making each competitor feel like a true ambassador for their country.

Fans eager to see what their nation's esports squad will be sporting won't have to wait long. The national team jerseys are expected to be unveiled in the coming weeks. For those who want to keep an eye on the reveals as they happen, the Esports Foundation has set up a dedicated hub at esportsnationscp.com/jerseys.
