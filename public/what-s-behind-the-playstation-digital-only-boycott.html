What's Behind the PlayStation Digital-Only Boycott?

PlayStation fans are organizing to make their voices heard, pushing back against Sony's perceived shift to a digital-only future for games.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Jul 28, 2026 · 10:56 UTC 2 MIN

A growing number of PlayStation gamers are organizing a boycott, driven by a perception that Sony is moving towards an all-digital future for its game releases. The movement aims to ensure Sony takes notice of their concerns, according to a report from GameRant in late July 2026.

This isn't the first time the digital-only approach has sparked conversation. In fact, a July 2026 article already highlighted "PlayStation's Digital-Only Future for Indie Devs" as a contentious issue, suggesting that developers, like players, have been wrestling with these changes for some time, GameRant noted.

The push for digital content runs parallel to broader industry discourse regarding physical versus digital distribution. A co-founder of Rockstar Games, for instance, expressed a personal affinity for physical media but indicated that they do not personally mind if games arrive on disc or not, as reported by PC Gamer.

Simultaneously, the digital landscape for games is becoming more complex for creators. New EU deepfake legislation, set to take effect on August 2nd, will require game developers to exercise greater caution when incorporating real-world figures and places into their titles, GamesIndustry.biz reported in late July 2026. This development adds another layer to the intricate considerations surrounding digital content creation and distribution.
