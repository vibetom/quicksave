What Are You Playing This Week? Your Guide To August's Busiest Release Schedule

From a record-breaking fighter to a cozy repair sim, here's everything hitting digital shelves between August 3rd and 9th.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Aug 03, 2026 · 16:55 UTC 3 MIN

This week, the gaming landscape is an open field, sprawling with new releases for every kind of player. Between August 3rd and 9th, 2026, we're seeing an impressive lineup, from high-octane blockbusters to quirky indie gems, ensuring that your backlog is about to get a whole lot longer.

Leading the charge is MARVEL Tōkon: Fighting Souls, set to launch on August 6th for $59.99. This fighter comes with a heavyweight reputation; its beta reportedly shattered Steam records, indicating a massive player base is already champing at the bit. For sports fans, the annual gridiron glory arrives with EA SPORTS Madden NFL 27, ready to kick off another season of virtual touchdowns.

Action and adventure are well-represented with a trio of exciting titles dropping on August 4th. Game Freak steps into the action RPG arena with Beast of Reincarnation, launching on PC and PlayStation 5. Meanwhile, those with a taste for mystery can dive into the moody detective adventure Kill The Shadow. And for players who prefer to explore with friends, the open-world, co-op campaign of Big Walk offers a vast landscape to traverse together.

Things take a turn for the unconventional with Anomaly President, an action roguelike that starts the week strong on August 3rd. If you’re looking to explore the darker corners of the game world, The Sinking City 2 is also slated for release this week, promising more unsettling investigations. For those who prefer large-scale strategic combat, Hell Let Loose Vietnam delivers a gritty military experience.

On the simulation front, two unique entries are set to arrive on August 6th. For a more laid-back experience, ReStory: Chill Electronics Repairs offers a job simulator that lets you unwind while fixing virtual gadgets. Conversely, IRON NEST: Heavy Turret Simulator provides a robust military simulation for players who prefer to command heavy artillery.

Rounding out this packed week are other intriguing titles such as the charming Fields of Mistria and the delightful Capy Castaway. No matter your gaming preference, this week’s diverse offerings guarantee there’s a new adventure waiting just for you.
