EWC 2026: A Trio of Champions Crowned as Competition Heats Up

Team Vision, Virtus.pro, and RvPLegend claim top honors in TFT, PUBG, and FC Pro, each earning a hefty share of the record-breaking $75 million prize pool.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Jul 27, 2026 · 21:37 UTC 3 MIN

The Esports World Cup 2026 in Paris has been a crucible of high-stakes competition, and this past week saw three more teams and players etch their names into the history books. As the tournament continues to unfold with its unprecedented $75 million prize pool across 25 tournaments and 24 games, the level of play has been nothing short of electrifying.

Team Vision kicked off the week's triumphs by asserting their dominance in the Teamfight Tactics (TFT) tournament. They delivered a commanding performance against Aurora Gaming in the Grand Final, sweeping the series 3-0. This decisive victory not only secured Team Vision the TFT title but also earned them a significant $150,000 in prize money from the total $500,000 pool. Beyond the cash, their win added 1,000 crucial Club Championship Points, propelling them to fourth place overall in the standings with 1,750 points.

Next, the battle royale arena of PUBG: Battlegrounds witnessed Virtus.pro rise to the occasion. Over a demanding period from July 21-26, Virtus.pro navigated the intense competition to claim the championship. Their triumph granted them a share of the substantial $2 million prize pool for the event and a vital 1,000 points toward the EWC Club Championship. This moved them into second place overall with 1,950 points, trailing only Natus Vincere, who currently hold 2,250 points. A standout performance came from Virtus.pro's Nino "NIXZYEE" Mikec, who was awarded the Sony MVP title. NIXZYEE's impressive stats, including 33 eliminations, 32 instances of incapacitating opponents, and staying in the fight for an average of around 21 minutes, earned him an additional $25,000.

The virtual pitch of EA SPORTS FC 26 also delivered a nail-biting conclusion in the FC Pro World Championship. Razvan "RvPLegend" Puiu of HYPERSPIRIT faced off against Levy "levyfinn" Rieck in a Grand Final that went down to the wire. In a thrilling display of skill, RvPLegend emerged victorious with a narrow 6-5 scoreline, securing the championship title and a quarter-million-dollar first-place prize of $250,000.

With the Esports World Cup 2026 continuing to captivate audiences until August 23, these recent championships underscore the diverse talent and incredible stakes at play. The competition is fierce, the rewards are immense, and the quest for the ultimate Club Championship title is heating up.
