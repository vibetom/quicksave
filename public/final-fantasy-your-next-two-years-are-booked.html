Final Fantasy: Your Next Two Years Are Booked

From a massive FFVII/FFXIV crossover to new platforms and the conclusion of the FFVII remake, here's everything Square Enix just announced.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Jul 25, 2026 · 15:36 UTC 3 MIN

Final Fantasy fans, clear your calendars. Square Enix has laid out a packed roadmap spanning the beloved franchise's past, present, and future, delivering a slew of significant announcements that promise to keep players busy well into 2027.

First up, a monumental crossover is officially on the horizon: Final Fantasy XIV and Final Fantasy VII are set to collide. Confirmed at the Final Fantasy XIV Fan Festival in Berlin, a new raid series titled 'Beyond the Lifestream' will bring elements of Gaia into the realm of Eorzea. This highly anticipated collaboration is slated to launch as part of the upcoming FFXIV: Evercold expansion, which is scheduled for release in January 2027.

Moving to the continuation of a modern saga, the final installment of the Final Fantasy VII remake trilogy now has a clearer path. Final Fantasy VII: Revelation is in development, promising an even grander open world than its predecessor, Final Fantasy VII: Rebirth. The conclusion to Cloud Strife's reimagined journey is currently on track for a Spring 2027 release, according to a statement from Square Enix.

For those looking to revisit a classic, Final Fantasy X is celebrating a major milestone. To commemorate the game's 25th anniversary, the Final Fantasy X/X-2 HD Remaster is making its way to the Nintendo Switch 2. Players can dive back into the world of Spira starting July 23, 2026, marking a new chapter for the iconic title on a new platform.

And for existing FFXIV players, the world of Eorzea is becoming more accessible than ever. Final Fantasy XIV will officially launch on the Nintendo Switch 2 on August 4. Current subscribers looking to expand their horizons can take advantage of a 50% discount on the new platform's version, with pre-orders for the game now available.

From grand new crossovers and the conclusion of an epic remake to classic re-releases and expanded platform accessibility, the next couple of years are shaping up to be an extraordinary time for Final Fantasy enthusiasts. Players have plenty to look forward to as Square Enix continues to build upon the enduring legacy of the series.
