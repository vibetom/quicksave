Xbox Achievements Are Getting a Platinum-Level Upgrade (And More!)

Reports suggest Xbox is finally rolling out its own version of the coveted Platinum Trophy, alongside other exciting new capture features for consoles.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Aug 06, 2026 · 10:59 UTC 3 MIN

Xbox players may soon have a new pinnacle to strive for in their gaming journeys. Reports indicate that Xbox is planning to introduce its own take on the PlayStation Platinum Trophy, a coveted prize for players who push games to their absolute limits. This potential addition could significantly evolve how players engage with achievements, offering a distinct mark of mastery for those who complete every challenge a game has to offer.

According to IGN, this new, higher tier of achievement would stand apart from the existing Gamerscore system, providing a dedicated reward for true completionists. It's a move many in the Xbox community have long hoped for, finally giving them a unique badge to display their deepest dives into game worlds.

GameSpot also reported on the teased Platinum-like trophy, suggesting it could arrive sometime later this year. This new system promises to be a robust recognition for players who dedicate themselves to fully conquering a game's achievement list.

Beyond this major achievement overhaul, Xbox is also confirmed to be rolling out new capture and achievement features across its consoles. While the specifics of these features haven't been fully detailed, IGN reports that these updates aim to enhance the overall player experience, making it easier and more rewarding to track progress and share gaming moments.

Together, these planned updates signal a significant refinement of the Xbox achievement ecosystem. By potentially adding a Platinum-like reward and bolstering its capture tools, Xbox appears focused on offering more comprehensive and satisfying ways for players to both track and display their gaming prowess. It's an exciting prospect for anyone who loves to chase every last unlockable and share their triumphs.
