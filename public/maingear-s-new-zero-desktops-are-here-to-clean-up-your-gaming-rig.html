MAINGEAR's New Zero Desktops Are Here to Clean Up Your Gaming Rig

Beyond the top-tier specs, the Zero series focuses on a minimalist design that hides cables and RGB, creating a panoramic, unobstructed view of your high-powered components.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Aug 02, 2026 · 12:34 UTC 3 MIN

MAINGEAR officially pulled back the curtain on its new 'Zero' gaming desktop series on July 30, 2026, signaling a fresh direction for their high-performance machines. While the name might suggest less, these rigs are designed to deliver top-tier power with an unprecedented focus on visual clarity.

The real showstopper here is the Zero series' commitment to a minimalist aesthetic. Imagine a gaming PC where the intricate dance of components isn't overshadowed by a spaghetti of cables or a cacophony of exposed RGB lights. MAINGEAR promises exactly this, incorporating sweeping glass panels that offer a clear look at the internals, with no visible motherboard connections or individual LED lights to distract. It's a design philosophy that champions a completely transparent view, free from visual clutter, creating a pristine window into your rig's heart.

But don't let the clean lines fool you into thinking these machines skimp on power. The Zero series is engineered to house some of the most formidable hardware available. Enthusiasts can configure these desktops with up to an AMD Ryzen 7 9850X3D CPU or an Intel Core Ultra 7 270K Plus, paired with an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090 GPU, ensuring they can tackle the most demanding games and applications with ease.

For those looking to step into this blend of high performance and elegant design, MAINGEAR is offering several starting configurations. The 'Zero | Emerald' model kicks off at $2,799, featuring an Intel Core Ultra 5 250K Plus processor alongside an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070 graphics card. Meanwhile, the 'Zero | Platinum' starts at $2,999, powered by an AMD Ryzen 7 7800X3D CPU and also equipped with an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070.

This launch underscores MAINGEAR's intention to push the boundaries not just on raw computing might, but also on how that power is presented. The Zero series is a clear statement that the company understands a segment of the high-end PC market values both extreme performance and a refined, visually striking design in their gaming setups. It’s a clean future for gaming rigs, and MAINGEAR is leading the charge.
