A Teen Champ and Mobile Legends: Bang Bang's Meteoric Rise at EWC 2026

From a 15-year-old making history in Street Fighter 6 to MLBB smashing viewership records and crowning unexpected champions, the Esports World Cup is delivering big moments and shifting the landscape of competitive gaming.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Aug 03, 2026 · 21:37 UTC 4 MIN

The Esports World Cup 2026, currently underway in Paris, France, is proving to be a crucible for new talent and a stage for mobile esports to flex its formidable muscles. Amidst the competition across 24 titles and a $75 million prize pool, we've seen a young phenom take a fighting game crown and Mobile Legends: Bang Bang plant its flag as an undeniable viewership titan.

Week 4 of the EWC saw the coronation of Craime, a 15-year-old sensation who became the youngest EWC champion in Street Fighter 6. It's a striking reminder that the competitive gaming landscape is constantly refreshed by new blood, ready to make their mark on the biggest stages.

But if there's one story dominating the EWC 2026, it's the sheer force of Mobile Legends: Bang Bang. The Mid Season Cup (MSC) for MLBB, a tournament historically dominated by South-east Asian teams, delivered a historic upset with Team Spirit emerging victorious. This marks the initial time a club from Eastern Europe and Central Asia has claimed the MSC title, overcoming Yangon Galacticos in a nail-biting 4-3 Grand Final. The win earned Team Spirit a cool $1 million and 1,000 Club Championship points, with Mathaios 'Kid Bomba' Chatzilakos earning the Sony Finals MVP award. This triumph is a seismic shift, demonstrating the growing global reach and competitive depth of MLBB.

Beyond the competitive drama, the MLBB MSC solidified its position as the undisputed viewership leader of EWC 2026. According to Esports Charts, the tournament garnered nearly double the peak audience observed for the League of Legends tournament, a major feat. This performance also placed the MLBB MSC third among all esports events held in 2026 so far, with only the M7 World Championship and MPL Indonesia Season 17 drawing larger audiences.

While the 2026 MSC's overall viewership metrics

including Peak Viewers, which decreased by 5.9%, Hours Watched, down 10.2%, and Average Viewers, seeing a 14.7% reduction

saw a slight dip compared to the previous year's edition, it still comfortably surpassed MSC 2024. This suggests a maturing but incredibly robust audience, proving that MLBB's appeal remains massive, even as individual event numbers fluctuate.

With most of the festival's strongest viewership draws now concluded, the EWC 2026, which runs through August 23, continues its intense Week 5. As lower bracket play determines the final four qualifiers leading into the August 5 Playoffs and August 8 Grand Final, only Counter-Strike and PUBG Mobile are considered realistic challengers to the MLBB MSC's impressive 2.89 million peak viewership. The Esports World Cup is undeniably showcasing both the raw talent of a new generation and the burgeoning power of mobile esports, with Mobile Legends: Bang Bang leading the charge.
