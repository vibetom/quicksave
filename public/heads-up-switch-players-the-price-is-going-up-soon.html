Heads Up, Switch Players: The Price Is Going Up Soon

Nintendo's Switch 2 is getting a $50 price bump in September, but a current bundle could save you even more if you act fast.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Aug 03, 2026 · 11:55 UTC 3 MIN

The cost of a Nintendo Switch 2 is about to climb, with a $50 price increase set to hit North America on September 1, 2026, pushing the console's price to $499 USD. This change isn't exclusive to the U.S. either; Canadian players will see the price jump from $629.99 CAD to $679.99 CAD, and European customers will face a rise from €469.99 to €499.99. Japan, however, will experience these shifts sooner, with the price of their Switch 2 model increasing from ¥49,980 to ¥59,980 on May 26, 2026. This earlier adjustment also extends to previous-generation Switch models, including the OLED, Lite, and V2, within Japan, alongside an increase in Nintendo Switch Online subscription costs for both Japan and South Korea, effective July 1, 2026.

Nintendo has attributed these widespread price adjustments to what it describes as "changes in market conditions." This includes the ongoing AI boom and geopolitical factors that are making component sourcing more challenging, according to IGN.

For those who've been on the fence about picking up a Switch 2, now might be the moment to act. Currently, the "Choose Your Game Bundle" offers the Switch 2 console for $499, bundled with a free digital title. Players can select from Mario Kart World, Donkey Kong Bananza, or Pok mon Pokopia. This effectively shaves $70-$80 off the total cost, as these games would normally be purchased separately. With the console's price set to rise to $499 on September 1, this bundle is currently described as being "up to $130 under upcoming price hike," meaning its overall value will soon jump to $580. This strategic timing encourages immediate purchases, allowing buyers to essentially secure the console at its current standalone price while receiving a free game.

This isn't an isolated incident in the hardware market. Other companies like Meta have recently raised the price of their Quest 3 VR headset, and Sony has increased the cost for all PlayStation 5 models, including refurbished PS5 Slims. Reports from earlier in May also indicated that Nintendo shareholders had been pushing for an increase in the Switch 2's price. For North American players, the window to grab a Switch 2 at its current price, and especially with the added value of a free game bundle, is closing as September 1 approaches.
