Can FaZe Clan Cement Its Legacy at the Esports World Cup?

The reigning CDL champions are favorites for the Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 title, but a field of 16 teams stands between them and esports immortality in Paris.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Aug 05, 2026 · 19:00 UTC 3 MIN

The Esports World Cup (EWC) 2026 is currently underway in Paris, France, hosting the Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 tournament, a high-stakes competition running from August 5th to August 9th. Sixteen teams are battling it out for a significant $1.8 million prize pool, and the stakes are particularly high for one reigning champion.

FaZe Clan arrives in Paris as the clear frontrunner, fresh off their victory at the Call of Duty League (CDL) Championship. With an implied probability of 40% to claim the EWC title, they're the team everyone is watching. Their recent CDL triumph saw Chris "Simp" Lehr, the Champs MVP, deliver a commanding performance, boasting a 1.11 K/D ratio across 19 maps. This consistent firepower makes them a formidable force in the competitive landscape.

For FaZe Clan, the EWC represents more than just another tournament win; it's a chance to etch their name into the history books. A victory here would solidify their status as one of the most dominant teams in the franchise's competitive era. Their journey at the EWC begins with a match against The Pit, an all-European roster that secured their spot through the Last Chance Qualifier. This opening bout sets the stage for what FaZe Clan hopes will be a historic run.

While FaZe Clan holds the spotlight, the tournament field is far from a one-horse race. According to an esports news site, primary contenders include familiar names like OpTic Gaming and 100 Thieves, both known for their competitive prowess. Additionally, Falcons and Gentle Mates have demonstrated strong past performances, making them significant threats. The tournament's blend of established powerhouses and ambitious amateur teams promises a dynamic and unpredictable fight for the Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 championship.
