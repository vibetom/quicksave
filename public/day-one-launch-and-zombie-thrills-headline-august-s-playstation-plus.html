Day-One Launch and Zombie Thrills Headline August's PlayStation Plus Lineup

Get ready to team up in a new co-op adventure or face the undead in a parkour-filled open world with this month's PlayStation Plus offerings.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Aug 02, 2026 · 10:13 UTC 2 MIN

PlayStation Plus subscribers are in for a treat this August 2026, with Sony rolling out a compelling trio of titles that blend established horror, unique indie co-op, and a beloved survival experience. Headlining the collection are Dying Light 2: Stay Human: Reloaded Edition and a day-one launch for Big Walk, the latest from the creators of Untitled Goose Game.

Big Walk, launching August 4th, arrives directly onto PlayStation Plus for PS5. This new co-operative open-world game, developed by the minds at House House, invites players to explore and solve puzzles together. Communication is key, as the game leverages proximity voice chat and gestures to foster teamwork in its expansive environments, according to GAMINGbible.

Meanwhile, Dying Light 2: Stay Human: Reloaded Edition offers PS4 and PS5 players a sprawling, parkour-fueled journey into a zombie-infested world. This edition promises a robust survival experience, where agility and combat prowess are equally vital for navigating its perilous landscapes.

Rounding out the August selection is Signalis for PS4, a critically acclaimed survival horror title that delivers a dose of classic genre tension. The lineup is further sweetened by a bonus Marvel T kon pack, adding extra value for subscribers, as reported by ComicBook.com.

This month's PlayStation Plus offering delivers a diverse array of experiences, from tackling the undead in a vast open world to embarking on a collaborative adventure with friends, ensuring there's something for every type of player.
