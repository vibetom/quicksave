One Chicken Dinner Could Change Everything at the PUBG Grand Finals

The Esports World Cup 2026 PUBG: Battlegrounds tournament concludes today in Paris, with over $2 million on the line and a dramatic 'Smash Rule' that means the championship could be decided in a single, final match.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Jul 25, 2026 · 12:14 UTC 3 MIN

The battle royale arena of PUBG: Battlegrounds is setting the stage for a thrilling conclusion at the Esports World Cup 2026 Grand Finals. Held in the vibrant city of Paris, France, the tournament has seen twenty-four elite teams duke it out since July 24, all vying for supremacy and a significant slice of a $2,025,000 prize pool. The action culminates today, July 26, promising an explosive finish that could reshape the leaderboard with a single, decisive moment.

Over the past three days, these teams have navigated a grueling schedule of 18 matches, each skirmish pushing them closer to the ultimate goal. The grand champions aren't just playing for bragging rights; the top team will walk away with a formidable $650,000, while an individual player will also be celebrated with a $25,000 MVP Award, recognizing standout performance amidst intense competition.

What makes this final day particularly electrifying is the tournament's unique 'Smash Rule.' As the Grand Finals wrap up, a team that has already reached match-point eligibility can instantly seize the championship by securing a 'Chicken Dinner' – the coveted win for surviving a match. If no match-point eligible team manages this feat, the victor will instead be determined by the highest total points accumulated across all 18 matches. This rule injects a layer of unpredictability, turning every late-game circle into a potential championship decider.

Competitors have been clashing in Squads TPP mode on PC, a format that demands seamless teamwork, strategic positioning, and precision gunplay. The stakes are immense, transforming each tactical decision and firefight into a critical juncture.

With the final results pending as of July 25, every fan will be glued to the screens today, July 26, as the remaining matches unfold. The 'Smash Rule' stands ready to turn the final moments into an unforgettable highlight, where one well-executed play could crown a champion and etch their name into the history of the Esports World Cup. The air in Paris is thick with anticipation, waiting to see which team will claim the ultimate victory.
