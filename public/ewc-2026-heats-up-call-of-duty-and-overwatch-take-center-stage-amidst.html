EWC 2026 Heats Up: Call of Duty and Overwatch Take Center Stage Amidst Million-Dollar Showdowns

The Esports World Cup is delivering non-stop action, with the Warzone Resurgence Championship and OWCS Midseason Championship currently underway, each boasting a $1,000,000 prize pool and crucial Club Championship points.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Jul 30, 2026 · 13:02 UTC 3 MIN

The Esports World Cup 2026 is an absolute whirlwind, with some of the biggest titles in competitive gaming now deep in the fray. Kicking off today, July 30, the Call of Duty: Warzone Resurgence Series 2026 Championship is set to ignite the battlefield. This high-stakes tournament, running through August 2, pits 32 formidable teams against each other, all vying for a share of a substantial $1,000,000 prize pool and vital Club Championship points.

But the action doesn't stop there. Running concurrently, the Overwatch Champions Series (OWCS) 2026 Midseason Championship is also currently underway at the EWC 26, having started July 29 and concluding on August 2. Here, 16 of the world's elite Overwatch squads are locked in a fierce battle for their own $1,000,000 prize pool and those coveted Club Championship points, which are paramount in the overall EWC narrative.

These ongoing spectacles build on a foundation of intense competition that has already seen champions crowned across various genres. Virtus.pro recently demonstrated a masterclass in consistency, emerging victorious in the PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS competition at the Esports World Cup 2026, besting 16 other teams over three days of intense play. On the tactical front, Team Vision delivered a dominant performance, sweeping Aurora Gaming 3-0 to claim the Teamfight Tactics championship at EWC 26, securing $150,000 and 1,000 Club Championship points for their efforts.

The individual skill shined brightly in the FC Pro World Championship, where Razvan “RvPLegend” Puiu hoisted the trophy. In a thrilling Grand Final, Puiu narrowly defeated Levy “levyfinn” Rieck with a 6-5 scoreline, earning himself $250,000. These diverse victories underscore the breadth and depth of the Esports World Cup, solidifying its position as a premier global event where top-tier talent competes for life-changing prize money and critical Club Championship standing.
