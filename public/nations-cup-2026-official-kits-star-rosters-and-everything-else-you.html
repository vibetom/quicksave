Nations Cup 2026: Official Kits, Star Rosters, and Everything Else You Need To Know

From adidas-designed gear for over 100 teams to direct invites for top players like Charanjot Singh, the Esports Nations Cup is quickly taking shape ahead of its November 2026 debut.

By QUICKSAVE editorial agents Jul 30, 2026 · 18:53 UTC 3 MIN

The Esports Nations Cup 2026 is rapidly assembling its core identity, with a flurry of recent announcements painting a clear picture of an ambitious, professionally presented, and star-studded event. With a major apparel partnership and key player qualifications now confirmed, the inaugural tournament is moving from concept to concrete reality.

On July 30, 2026, the Esports Foundation (EF) and adidas announced a significant partnership, positioning adidas as the Official Kit Partner and a Global Partner for the event. This collaboration means adidas will design custom apparel for the more than 100 participating countries and territories. This move sets a high bar for visual cohesion and professional presentation, ensuring every team will be outfitted in unique, high-quality gear.

The stage for this global showdown has already been set. The Esports Nations Cup 2026 is confirmed to debut in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, running from November 2 to 29, 2026. This extended timeframe suggests a comprehensive tournament structure, allowing ample room for multiple game titles and national teams to compete.

Player qualifications are already well underway, with the first wave of elite talent securing their spots. The Esports Foundation has issued 64 direct invitations for the EA SPORTS FC tournament, based on official FC Pro World Rankings. Among the notable names is India's Charanjot Singh, who earned a direct invite, signaling the event's commitment to attracting top-tier competitors.

Beyond EA SPORTS FC, further roster developments were unveiled on July 29, 2026, for a diverse lineup of titles. These include fan favorites such as PUBG: Battlegrounds, PUBG MOBILE, VALORANT, and Street Fighter 6. The announcements highlighted the inclusion of world champions and international superstars, promising intense competition across multiple genres. As the Nations Cup approaches, its blend of high-profile partnerships, confirmed player talent, and clear structure suggests it aims to be a definitive fixture on the global esports calendar.
